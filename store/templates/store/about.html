PLANT CARE GUIDE

Plant Care, Made Simple.

New to plants? No worries. Open any topic below to get a quick, simple summary of the care your plants need.

Summary: Most indoor plants prefer bright, indirect light. Avoid placing sensitive plants directly under harsh afternoon sunlight.
GreenNest Tip: If the leaves look pale or growth becomes slow, check whether your plant is receiving enough light.
Summary: Don't follow a fixed watering schedule blindly. Check the soil first. If the top layer feels dry, your plant may need water.
GreenNest Tip: Overwatering is one of the most common causes of indoor plant problems.
Summary: Use a suitable potting mix and make sure excess water can escape through drainage holes. Waterlogged soil can damage roots.
GreenNest Tip: A beautiful pot is great, but proper drainage is even more important.
Summary: Many tropical plants enjoy moderate humidity. Dry indoor air can sometimes lead to brown leaf edges.
GreenNest Tip: Grouping plants together can help create a slightly more humid micro-environment.
Summary: Plants need nutrients for healthy growth. Follow the fertilizer instructions for your specific plant and avoid excessive feeding.
GreenNest Tip: More fertilizer does not always mean faster growth.
Summary: Remove dead or damaged leaves with clean tools. Gently cleaning dusty leaves can also help keep your plant looking fresh.
GreenNest Tip: Always use clean scissors when pruning your plants.
