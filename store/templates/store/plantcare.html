Care Essentials

Everything your plant needs

A few simple habits can make a big difference. Follow these essential care practices to keep your plants fresh, healthy and beautiful.

Watering

Check the soil before watering. Give enough water to keep the roots hydrated without leaving the soil soggy.

Sunlight

Every plant has different light needs. Place your plant where it receives the right amount of bright, indirect or direct light.

Fresh Air

Good air circulation keeps leaves fresh and helps reduce excess moisture around your plants.

Love & Care

Clean dusty leaves, remove dead foliage and regularly check your plant for changes in colour, texture or growth.

Green Routine

A little care every day

You don't need to spend hours caring for your plants. A few mindful checks are enough to keep your indoor garden thriving.

01
Check the soil

Touch the top layer of soil. If it feels dry, your plant may be ready for watering.

02
Observe the leaves

Look for yellowing, drooping, spots or dusty leaves and respond early when something changes.

03
Check the light

Make sure your plant is receiving the amount of light suitable for its variety.

04
Give it some attention

Rotate the pot occasionally, remove dead leaves and keep the growing area clean.
