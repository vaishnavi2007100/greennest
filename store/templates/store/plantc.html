The Essentials

Simple Care. Happy Plants.

Four simple things make the biggest difference in the everyday health of your plants.

Watering

Check the top layer of soil before watering. Water thoroughly when the soil feels dry and always avoid standing water.

Sunlight

Every plant has different light requirements. Place your plant where it receives the right amount of natural light.

Airflow

Good airflow keeps the growing environment fresh and helps prevent excess moisture around the foliage.

Nutrition

Give suitable plant nutrients during active growth according to the needs of your particular plant.

Easy Routine

Your Weekly Plant Routine

Spend a few minutes every week with your plants. Consistency is more important than complicated care.

01

Check the Soil

Touch the upper layer of soil and check whether the plant actually needs water.

02

Inspect Leaves

Look for dust, yellow leaves or unusual spots and remove damaged foliage carefully.

03

Rotate Plant

Rotate your indoor plant occasionally so different sides receive light evenly.

04

Clean & Refresh

Gently clean dusty leaves and keep the surrounding area fresh and tidy.

GreenNest Tips

Little Things Matter.

Check Before You Water

Don't follow a fixed watering schedule. Check the soil first.

Observe Your Plant

Leaves often tell you when something about light or watering needs attention.

Remove Damaged Leaves

Regularly removing clearly damaged foliage keeps the plant looking tidy.

Be Consistent

Small regular care is better than occasional intensive care.

Avoid These

Common Plant-Care Mistakes

Sometimes plants struggle because of small mistakes that are easy to avoid.

Overwatering

Watering too frequently can leave the soil constantly wet and may damage the roots.

Wrong Light

Keeping a plant in unsuitable light can affect its growth and appearance.

Ignoring Soil

Poor drainage or unsuitable soil can make watering and root health more difficult.

No Leaf Cleaning

Dust can build up on leaves, so gently cleaning them from time to time is useful.

Too Much Fertilizer

More fertilizer does not always mean more growth. Follow suitable plant guidance.

Forgetting Observation

Regularly observing your plants helps you notice changes before they become serious.

Throughout The Year

Seasonal Plant Care

Your plants may need slightly different attention as temperature and light conditions change.

Summer

Keep an eye on soil moisture and protect sensitive plants from harsh direct heat.

  • Check soil regularly
  • Avoid extreme heat
  • Maintain airflow

Monsoon

Higher humidity can reduce how often some plants need watering.

  • Avoid excess water
  • Improve ventilation
  • Watch for fungal issues

Winter

Growth may slow during cooler periods, so watering may need adjustment.

  • Reduce excess watering
  • Provide suitable light
  • Protect from cold drafts

Spring

Many plants become more active as conditions become warmer and brighter.

  • Check new growth
  • Refresh soil if needed
  • Resume active care
